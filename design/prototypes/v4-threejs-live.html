Questions

Before you order.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Ask within 30 days of sign-up and we refund the hosting fee for your first service. Domain fees, setup fees and design work are not refundable.

Are prices monthly or yearly?

cPanel hosting is billed yearly. VPS servers are billed monthly. Every price on this site shows its billing period.

Can I move my site from another host?

Yes. Open a ticket after you order and our team will help you move your files, databases and email.

Can I upgrade from cPanel hosting to a VPS later?

Yes. When your site outgrows shared hosting, we move you to a VPS without changing providers.
