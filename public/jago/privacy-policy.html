1. What Jago does

Jago is a challenge alarm app. You can set alarms and use walking, camera focus, math or memory challenges. No Jago account is required. There are no ads, purchases, app-owned analytics or push-update services in this version.

2. Information stored on your device

Alarm times, repeat days, dates, challenge settings, preferences, completion history and bounded diagnostic records are stored in the app's private storage. If you choose a custom sound, Jago stores its document reference and access permission, not a copy uploaded to a server. These records are used to deliver alarms and show your progress.

3. Camera and walking

Camera permission is used only for a visible camera-focus challenge. Camera frames are processed on your device with Google's bundled ML Kit face-detection library. Jago does not save photos or video, upload camera frames, identify you, or create a biometric identity profile. Focus is an approximate eyes-open/head-position check, not proof of wakefulness or true gaze tracking. Natural blinking pauses counting without erasing completed progress.
Physical activity permission allows the device step counter to count steps for a walking challenge. Jago does not request your location or microphone. You can choose a math or memory challenge if you do not wish to use these permissions. Revoke permissions in Android Settings; doing so may prevent an already configured challenge from working.

4. Network and third-party software

This release removes INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions from the final app manifest, including permissions contributed by libraries. Jago cannot make direct app-network connections. Alarm scheduling and bundled camera processing do not require a connection. The app includes AndroidX, CameraX, Google ML Kit and bundled Three.js for decorative animations. ML Kit's general documentation describes diagnostic/usage collection; this build restricts direct SDK network transmission through the app's permission configuration. We do not operate a Jago data-collection server.
If you open the developer website, email support, or select a document from another provider, that browser, email app or document provider operates under its own privacy rules. Android and Google Play services may process system/store information under their own policies.

5. Backup and sharing

Automatic app backup and app data transfer are excluded by this release's Android backup rules. Jago does not sell your data or include advertising SDKs. Device/OS behavior outside the app's control is governed by your device settings and provider policies.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete individual alarms in Jago. To delete all locally stored Jago data and history, use Android Settings > Apps > Jago > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This cannot be undone and removes your alarms. Removing a sound from Jago does not delete the original audio file. You control that file through its document provider.

7. Support and changes

If you contact info@raishahost.com, information you voluntarily include will be processed to respond to your request. Do not send face images, identity documents or other sensitive information for routine support. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change. New tracking, online backup or push features are not covered as if already enabled.

8. Permissions and device limits

Notification, exact-alarm and full-screen access support scheduled alarm delivery. Alarms may be affected by force-stop, power-off, revoked permissions and device restrictions. Jago is not a medical or emergency service. Choose challenge settings appropriate for your circumstances.
